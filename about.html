Our Story

A journey of hospitality, heritage, and heart.

A Warm Welcome, Always

Tsholofelo Guest Lodge was founded with a simple vision: to create a place where visitors to Gaborone feel not just accommodated, but truly cared for.

The name Tsholofelo means "hope" in Setswana — a value that guides everything we do. From the warm smiles at reception to the fresh breakfast served each morning, we treat every guest as part of our family.

Our lodge is proudly Motswana-owned and operated. We employ local staff, source from local suppliers, and showcase the very best of Botswana hospitality to every visitor who walks through our doors.

Whether you're visiting for business, family, or a peaceful escape, we promise a stay that feels like home.
